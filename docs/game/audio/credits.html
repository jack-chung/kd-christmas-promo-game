Wrap Station music

Three old favourites, freshly arranged for your wrapping band. You play the melody!

  • Deck the Halls — traditional Welsh melody “Nos Galan,” published with English words in 1862.
  • O Christmas Tree — traditional German melody, associated with Ernst Anschütz’s 1824 song. Our arrangement uses a gentle four-beat pulse.
  • Joy to the World — ANTIOCH, Lowell Mason (1792–1872), 1839. Complete traditional melody checked against Mutopia’s public-domain score, transposed to C with a four-beat pulse.

Only the public-domain melodies are used. Accompaniment and game charts were made for The Tuneville Gift. No lyrics or modern recordings of these songs are used.

Marimba, pizzicato violin section, concert harp, glockenspiel and pizzicato double bass: VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner and contributors. Recordings released under CC0 1.0 Universal. Trimmed, normalized and converted for this game. Thank you to the musicians and recordists!

Sorting Office music

Good King Wenceslas — the traditional “Tempus adest floridum” melody from Piae Cantiones (1582). Complete melody transposed to G major from Mutopia’s public-domain edition of Hutchins, Carols Old and Carols New (1916), no. 415. No lyrics are used. Our own arrangement alternates marimba and harp melodies over pizzicato strings, bass and a light kit, with three verse textures and a gentle stage lift.

The same CC0 recordings credited above supply the marimba, harp, strings and bass; there are no new recordings or audio downloads for this arrangement.

Sorting landings use the same recorded marimba and harp, with a little original woody pop. Each sack has its own note in G major. String fanfares, harp combo echoes and the original results flourish also use this CC0 bank. The comic spring and paper-rasp effects are original generated buffers.

The kick, snare, shaker and warm pad are original generated sounds. Other game cues are original synthesis.
